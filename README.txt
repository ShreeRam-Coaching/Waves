WAVES GYM - cinematic scroll website

WHAT'S INSIDE
  public/index.html   the whole website (styling and animation are inside this one file)
  public/media/       your Flow video (re-encoded for smooth scrolling), stills taken from it, and 48 backup frames

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (Hatchable)
  1. Create a new Hatchable project.
  2. Upload everything inside the "public" folder, keeping the "media" folder as it is.
  3. Press Deploy, then open the link.

CONTENT

  and the training types (strength, free weights, resistance machines, cardio, Studio Cycling, Yoga, Zumba, HIIT).
  There is no booking form. Visitors are asked to call the gym. All pictures are stills from your own video.
